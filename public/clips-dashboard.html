FAQ

Do I need to keep the tab open?

Yes, keep this tab open until the clips finish rendering.

What happens to my clips after 24 hours?

They are deleted automatically, 24 hours after generation. Download what you want to keep.

Why did I get fewer than 12 clips?

The AI only cuts moments it scores as strong. A shorter or quieter video may yield fewer clips.
